Fuzzy C-Means

          

            
            
            
            
Each point belongs to every cluster with different weights. Transparency shows how strong the main membership is.

            

              
              
            

          

        


        

          
Mean Shift

          

            
            
            
            
Visual only.

            
Moves each point toward the local mean of neighbors within the bandwidth and merges nearby final modes.

            

              
              
            

          

        


        

          
GMM

          

            
            
            
GMM uses EM: it estimates weight, mean, and covariance for each Gaussian and assigns each point to the most likely component.

            

              
              
            

          

        


        

          
DBSCAN

          

            
            
            
            
Visual only.

            
The epsilon range is drawn with gray circles: the more points fall inside the radius, the stronger the outline becomes.

            

              
              
            

          

        


        

          
HDBSCAN

          

            
            
            
Educational version: it uses core distance, mutual reachability, MST, and a hierarchical cut. Points outside minimum clusters become noise.
